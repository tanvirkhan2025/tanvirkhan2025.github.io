About

Engineer by training, educator by calling.

Md. Tanvir Khan is a GRE instructor, academic team lead, and engineer-turned-educator based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. He holds a B.Sc. in Food Engineering and Tea Technology from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST) and an MBA from the Institute of Business Administration (IBA), University of Dhaka.

Over the past six years, Tanvir has guided thousands of students toward their target GRE scores while also mentoring IBA-MBA aspirants and tutoring Math, Physics, Chemistry, and English. He founded and personally mentors Nishchit, his own GRE prep program; leads the trainer team at NetCom Learning Bangladesh delivering corporate training on Generative AI and leadership; and founded LearnSmart, a web-based learning platform. He's also an active AI-tool user (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, and more), weaving them into how he builds course content, trains corporate teams, and personalizes feedback.
